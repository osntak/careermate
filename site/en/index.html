Job analysis and cover letters, kept on your PC

Tell Claude or Codex "analyze this posting" or "draft a cover letter" and the result is saved in CareerMate. Everything is saved on your own computer.

How it works

You talk to your AI app. The results show up in a dashboard in your browser.

Add your resume

Have the AI read your resume or career document once. You don't upload it again for the next posting.

Analyze a posting

Give it a job link or the text. It compares the requirements with your background and notes strengths, gaps, and the evidence for each.

Cover letters and interviews

Cover letter versions, application status, and likely interview questions are filed under each posting.

Web and mobile AI apps can't reach local tools on your computer, so they aren't supported.

Your data is stored on your PC

The dashboard server binds only to 127.0.0.1 (this computer), so it can't be reached from outside.
CareerMate itself sends out only an update check (you can turn it off) and, when you search for jobs, the search keywords and role filters. It never sends your resume, cover letters, or applications. If you connect External storage yourself, data syncs to that folder, but never to a CareerMate server. The analysis and writing are done by the AI app you chose, and whatever goes to that AI service falls under its own policy.

  • Storage ~/.careermate/careermate.sqlite
  • Export cover letters and more to ~/.careermate/exports
  • Delete & manage right in the dashboard Settings
  • External storage only if you turn it on or ask your AI, syncs to a folder you pick (how)
